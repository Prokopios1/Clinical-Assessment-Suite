# Store Listing Details

**App Name**: Clinical Assessment
**Short Description**: Professional mental health assessments: PHQ-9, GAD-7, Personality & Psychosis Risk.

**Full Description**:
Clinical Assessment is a professional tool designed for clinicians and patients to track mental health progress using standardized scales.

Key Features:
- **Standardized Tests**:
  - **PHQ-9**: Depression Scale
  - **GAD-7**: Anxiety Scale
  - **PID-5-BF**: Brief Personality Inventory (25 items)
  - **MSI-BPD**: Borderline Personality Disorder Screening
  - **PQ-B**: Prodromal Questionnaire for Psychosis Risk
- **Track Progress**: Monitor scores over time with intuitive charts and history.
- **Secure Data**: Patient data is stored securely.
- **Clinician Dashboard**: Manage multiple patients and review assessment results efficiently.

Designed for ease of use and clinical accuracy. Download now to streamline your clinical assessment workflow.
